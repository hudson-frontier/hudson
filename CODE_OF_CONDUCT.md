# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience, give specific and constructive feedback, and focus
disagreements on the work. Respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and sustained
personal attacks are unacceptable in Hudson issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other project spaces. Do not publish another person's private information.

Report misconduct using GitHub's **Report content** action on the relevant
content. You may also contact a maintainer privately through contact information
they have chosen to publish on their GitHub profile. Avoid public reports that
expose private information. Maintainers will review reports, respect
confidentiality as far as practical, and may remove content, warn participants,
or restrict participation according to severity and repeated behavior.

These expectations apply to maintainers as well as contributors. Maintainers
involved in a report should step aside from handling it.
